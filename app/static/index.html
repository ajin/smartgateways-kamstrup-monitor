Heat on/off

Every time heat came in. Heat comes in when the heat valve in your delivery set opens: hot water then flows through the meter. That happens when the heating asks for heat, or when a hot tap or the shower runs: the meter measures both together. The dashboard recognises which part was probably hot tap water from the pattern in the readings.

How does the dashboard know?

It doesn't know for sure. Your meter measures all heat going into the heat unit, and that unit makes both the heating and the hot tap water. There is no separate reading for each. So the dashboard looks at the pattern, every time a reading comes in:

  • It learns how your heating behaves. While only the heating runs, the dashboard keeps a moving average (about 40 minutes) of its usual flow (for example ~220 l/h), its power and the temperature of the water going back. Short heating bursts that start with cooler water are normal heating too.
  • Hot tap water looks different. When a shower or hot tap runs, cold tap water cools the heat unit, so the water going back gets very cold (often 20–30°, colder than the heating ever sends back). The flow also changes: much lower than usual when only the tap takes heat, or a sudden jump above it when the tap runs on top of the heating.
  • It counts the right part. When only the tap takes heat, all of it is hot tap water. When the heating runs at the same time, only the heat above the heating's usual power is counted.
  • It rates how sure it is. Very cold water going back and a big change in flow for a few minutes is very likely hot tap water; a weaker sign is likely or possibly. Anything longer than 45 minutes is taken to be the heating.

The meter reads about once a minute, so a tap that runs for less than a minute may be missed. For an exact split you would need a second meter on the hot tap water.

About Kamstrup Monitor

      

        
A dashboard for a Kamstrup MULTICAL heat meter, read through the Kamst-IR gateway from Smart Gateways. It runs entirely in your home: it asks the gateway for the meter's values on your own network and keeps every reading on this computer.

        
Not a Kamstrup or Smart Gateways product

        
This is an independent project. It is not made, supported or endorsed by Kamstrup, Smart Gateways or your energy company. Kamstrup and MULTICAL are trademarks of their owners and are named only to say which meter this works with.

        
Use at your own risk

        
The software is provided as is, without any warranty. It only reads the gateway and never changes anything on it or on the meter. With sign-in switched off (Settings › Sign-in), everyone on your network can open the dashboard; keep it on a network you trust. Your energy company's bill and the meter's own display are always the reference; costs shown here are an estimate from the price you enter and leave out fixed charges.

What the words mean

        
    
          
  • Heat used (GJ or kWh): the energy your home took from the hot water, the number on your bill. 1 GJ is about 278 kWh.
    • 
          
  • Heat coming in (kW): how fast heat is used right now.
    • 
          
  • Hot water in and water going back: the temperatures of the water arriving from the district heating and leaving your home (supply and return).
    • 
          
  • Heat taken out: the difference between those two. The bigger, the better the heat is used.
    • 
          
  • Flow: litres of hot water per hour flowing through the meter.
